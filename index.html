WhatsApp Link Generator FAQ

What is a WhatsApp Link Generator?

A WhatsApp link generator is a free tool that creates a direct chat link (wa.me link) from any phone number. Instead of asking someone to save your number first, you simply share the link, and they are taken straight into a WhatsApp chat with you. It is used by businesses, freelancers, and content creators who want to make it as easy as possible for people to reach them on WhatsApp.

How to Create a WhatsApp Link

Creating a WhatsApp link takes less than 10 seconds:

  1. Enter your phone number with your country code (e.g. +971 for UAE)
  2. Add an optional pre-filled message your contact will see when they open the chat
  3. Copy your generated wa.me link or click "Open WhatsApp" to test it instantly

No signup required. No data stored. Works on any device.

What is a wa.me Link?

A wa.me link is WhatsApp's official short link format. When someone clicks it, WhatsApp opens directly to a chat with that number, without needing to save the contact first. You can also include a pre-filled message that appears in the chat box automatically.

https://wa.me/971501234567 https://wa.me/971501234567?text=Hi%2C%20I%20would%20like%20to%20chat
Is this WhatsApp link generator free?

Yes, completely free with no limits, no ads, and no signup required. Just enter your number and generate your link instantly.

Is my phone number stored anywhere?

No. Everything is processed locally in your browser only. Your phone number and message are never sent to or stored on any server.

Does the person need to save my number to use the link?

No. That is the whole point. When someone clicks your wa.me link, WhatsApp opens directly into a chat with you, with no contact saving needed.

Can I add a pre-filled message to my WhatsApp link?

Yes. Type your message in the "Optional message" field before generating your link. The recipient will see it pre-filled in the chat box when they click your link.

Does this work for WhatsApp Business?

Yes. WhatsApp links work the same way for both regular WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business accounts.

Which countries are supported?

All countries are supported. Just make sure to include your full country code when entering your phone number (e.g. +1 for USA, +44 for UK, +971 for UAE).
